## [0.0.1] - 2026-06-10

### Added

- Bootstrap: workspace skeleton with `crates/acu-core`.
- Cognitive core brick 1: `MindState` event-sourced replay, `step` cycle, `Substrate` and `EventStore` ports, `ReflexSubstrate` and `InMemoryEventStore` adapters.
- Governance: `.editorconfig`, `.gitattributes`, `deny.toml`, `lefthook.yml`, `cog.toml`, `release.toml`, CI/CD workflows, issue and PR templates.
